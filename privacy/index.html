No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Fonts are hosted on the same server, no content is loaded from third parties.

Light or dark mode

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to a server. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser data.

External links

This website links to GitHub, LinkedIn and other sites. Nothing is loaded from them until you click a link. Once you follow one, the privacy policy of that site applies.
