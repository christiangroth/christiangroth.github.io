PrinciplesHow I work

The rules everything above is built with.

  1. Done means done right.Shipped and running, on a foundation that doesn't lead into a dead end. Quick & dirty is neither.
  2. Boring tech wins.Proven tools first. New stuff only where it really pays off.
  3. Make decisions explicit.ADRs and arc42 next to the code, so the why outlives the people.
  4. Evolve, don't rewrite.Old or new, every step is shippable.
  5. Teams over tools.Architecture is a social activity. The best diagram is the one the team draws together.
  6. Automate the boring parts.Done twice? Script it. Done three times? Put it into CI.
